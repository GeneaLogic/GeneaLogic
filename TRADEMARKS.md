# Trademark policy

The code is open source under Apache-2.0, and the format spec under CC BY 4.0. Neither
license grants any rights in the **GeneaLogic** name; Apache-2.0 says so in §6. This
page says what you can do with it anyway. Its purpose is to keep users from being
misled about what they are running, and it is not meant to stop anyone from building on
the project.

The mark is unregistered and is held by Rafi Goldberg, the project's maintainer.

## You may, without asking

- **Refer to the project truthfully**: in articles, talks, reviews, package indexes, and
  "built with GeneaLogicKit" or "a fork of GeneaLogic" credits.
- **Redistribute an unmodified build** under the GeneaLogic name, including through a
  package manager.
- **Say that other software reads or writes GeneaLogic archives**, or uses the GeneaLogic
  archive format. The format identifier (`genealogic-archive-1.0`) and the `.genea`
  extension are technical identifiers: any implementation that conforms to the spec may
  declare them.

## You may not

- **Distribute a modified build under the GeneaLogic name.** A fork is welcome. It needs
  its own name and its own bundle identifier.
- **Use a confusingly similar name** for genealogy or archival software.
- **Imply endorsement** by, or affiliation with, the project when there is none.

If you're unsure, or want to do something this page doesn't cover, open an issue and ask.
